One Checklist. One Team. From Clearance to Wrap.

The six steps for filming in Zimbabwe, and Malachite handles each of them with you.

  1. Ministry of Information: Clearance Before Travel

    Apply through the Zimbabwe Embassy or directly to the Ministry, with a letter to the Permanent Secretary giving each person’s full name, gender and passport number, your organisation, designations and contacts, the assignment, exact dates and locations, and a copy of each press card. The clearance is needed for your visa.

  2. Zimbabwe Media Commission (ZMC): Accreditation

    Visit the ZMC in Harare on arrival: accreditation is per project. Bring your clearance letter and two passport photographs per person, and carry your accreditation at all times while filming.

  3. Location Permits: What You Film

    Wildlife and parks: a ZimParks permit, with extra fees for Hwange, Mana Pools, the Victoria Falls Rainforest and night or moonlight filming. Heritage: a National Museums and Monuments permit for Great Zimbabwe, the Matobo Hills, rock art sites and similar. Food, people and private places: written permission from the owner and signed consent and release forms, especially for children.

  4. Equipment: ZIMRA Temporary Import

    A full equipment list with serial numbers and values, cleared through a bonded agent or a temporary import guarantee. Keep the list for exit: all equipment must leave with the crew.

  5. Drones: CAAZ

    Separate authorisation from the Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe. Drone use is restricted in national parks, so apply in advance.

  6. Visa and Crew Documents

    Passports valid for at least six months with three empty pages, and a visa issued with the clearance letter. A crew list, flight details and an address in Zimbabwe.

Fees and processing times change. We confirm the current requirements, fees and timings in your quotation.
