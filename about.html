About Malachite

Travel packages across Zimbabwe, Southern Africa and East Africa, from offices in Harare, Victoria Falls and Kariba.

Who we are

Malachite Travel & Tours offers travel experiences across Zimbabwe, Southern Africa and East Africa, for wildlife enthusiasts, history lovers and cultural explorers alike.

Malachite Travel & Tours is based in Zimbabwe, with offices in Harare, Victoria Falls and Kariba. We offer packages in Zimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda, tailored to suit every type of traveller.

Our expert guides fill every moment of the journey with adventure, knowledge and memories worth keeping.

  • Our officesHarare, Victoria Falls and Kariba (Kariba office: Stand 643, ZV Mall)
  • Where we travelZimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda
  • Who we travel withWildlife enthusiasts, history lovers and cultural explorers

What we arrange

The travel experiences Malachite offers.

  • Photographic safarisGame drives in Zimbabwe's national parks, including Hwange and Matusadona, and cruising on Lake Kariba.
  • History and cultureHeritage sites such as the Great Zimbabwe Ruins and the rock paintings of the Matobo Hills.
  • Museums and monumentsLandmarks including National Heroes Acre in Harare, the Bulawayo Railway Museum and the Natural History Museum.
  • Victoria FallsThe Falls, with Chobe National Park in Botswana and Livingstone in Zambia easily accessible on day trips.
  • Lake Kariba houseboat chartersCruises past the Matusadona mountains, with walking safaris, island camping and tiger-fish and bream fishing.
  • Multi-country packagesPackages across Zimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda.
  • TransfersSeamless transfers by air, water and road, so every part of the journey is hassle-free.
