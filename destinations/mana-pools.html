Mana Pools

A Zambezi river wilderness shaped by water, floodplains and wildlife.

Along the Zambezi

Mana Pools is part of a remarkable river and floodplain wilderness in northern Zimbabwe. The Zambezi forms its northern boundary, while the wider World Heritage property includes the Sapi and Chewore Safari Areas.

The name Mana means “four” in Shona and refers to four permanent pools formed by former channels of the Zambezi. The landscape and wildlife are closely linked to the river and its seasonal floodplain.

Malachite's published Lower Zambezi journey features canoeing and walking-safari experiences as possible elements of a wider route. Specific activities, licensed guiding arrangements, access and availability must be confirmed for each enquiry.
