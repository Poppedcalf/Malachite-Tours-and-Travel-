Lake Kariba

A journey shaped by open water, shoreline wildlife and time on the lake.

Life on the water

Lake Kariba invites a slower kind of journey: open water, changing light, time along the shoreline and the freedom to shape each day around your group.

Malachite's published trip information includes houseboat bookings, boat cruises, tender-boat wildlife viewing and tiger-fishing experiences. Houseboat stays can be discussed for different trip lengths, with the vessel, route and arrangements confirmed for each request.

  • Houseboat staysAsk about overnight charter options and the facilities available on the proposed vessel.
  • Time on the waterDiscuss cruises, shoreline exploration and sunset outings.
  • Wildlife and fishingAsk about guided wildlife viewing and fishing options for your dates.
  • Private groupsShare your group size, preferred pace and any special requirements.

Build your time on Lake Kariba

A good enquiry starts with a few practical details. These help the team understand the kind of lake experience you have in mind.

  • Your datesShare your preferred travel dates or the period you are considering.
  • Your groupTell us how many adults and children may be travelling.
  • Trip lengthFor a houseboat request, indicate how many nights you are considering.
  • Your interestsLet the team know whether you are interested in relaxing on the water, wildlife viewing, fishing or a combination.
