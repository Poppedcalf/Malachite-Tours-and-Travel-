Heritage of Zimbabwe

Stone cities, rock art and cultural history across Zimbabwe.

Places that carry Zimbabwe's story

Zimbabwe's heritage is written into stone cities, archaeological sites, rock art, museums and living cultural traditions. A heritage journey makes space to understand the places as well as see them.

Great Zimbabwe National Monument is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. UNESCO describes the monument as an important centre of the Shona civilisation and trading network, with major areas known as the Hill Ruins, Great Enclosure and Valley Ruins. The main structures were built between approximately 1100 and 1450 AD.

Malachite's published heritage route links Bulawayo's Natural History Museum, Khami Ruins, San rock art in the Matobo Hills and Great Zimbabwe. The sequence, specialist guiding and visit arrangements should be confirmed for the dates requested.

  • Great ZimbabweExplore the stone architecture and the history of a major pre-colonial settlement.
  • Khami RuinsInclude another important archaeological site in a wider heritage route.
  • Matobo HillsDiscuss rock-art visits and the granite landscapes of the area.
  • BulawayoConsider a museum visit as part of the journey.
