Questions

Short answers about planning with Malachite.

How are itineraries arranged?

Choose a package, or tell us what you have in mind. Share your dates, group size and preferences, and Malachite prepares a proposal for you to review. Itineraries on this site are outlines; routing, inclusions and pricing are confirmed in your proposal.

How is pricing arranged?

Every journey is priced on request. Your price depends on your dates, group size, accommodation and extras, so the team prepares a quotation for you and confirms what is included.

Does sending an inquiry reserve my trip?

No. The buttons open WhatsApp or your email app with a prepared message that you review and send yourself. Arrangements are confirmed directly with Malachite.

How are houseboat trips arranged?

Tell us your dates, group size and what you would like to do on Lake Kariba, such as walking safaris, island camping or fishing for tiger fish and bream. Length, vessel details, capacity and availability are confirmed by Malachite in your proposal.

How do I ask about a group trip?

Use the Ask about a group trip button in the Contact us directly section. It opens a WhatsApp or email message where you can add your dates, group size and plans. Malachite confirms what is possible and the price.

Can I travel beyond Zimbabwe?

Yes. Malachite offers packages in South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda as well as Zimbabwe. The Mala Trans Borders Pack covers Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe, Chobe National Park in Botswana and Livingstone in Zambia.

What happens to a voluntary contribution?

Choosing one only adds it to your inquiry so the team can discuss it with you. This website does not collect money, and how contributions are handled is for Malachite to confirm.
