Zimbabwe, Southern and East Africa

Malachite Travel & Tours is proudly based in Zimbabwe, offering unforgettable packages to the most loved tourism hotspots across Zimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda.

Zimbabwe

The Heart of Africa’s Wonder.

From the mighty Victoria Falls and the wildlife of Hwange to the ancient Great Zimbabwe and the peaceful waters of Lake Kariba: a land of wonder, warmth and unforgettable adventure.

Explore Zimbabwe

South Africa

One Country, A World of Wonders.

From the iconic Table Mountain and vibrant Cape Town to the Big Five in Kruger National Park and the scenic Garden Route: history, wildlife and breathtaking beauty all in one journey.

Explore South Africa

Botswana

Where the Wild Still Roams Free.

From the winding waterways of the Okavango Delta to the elephant herds of Chobe, experience untouched wilderness, luxury safaris and nature at its purest.

Explore Botswana

Tanzania

Where Legends Are Born.

From the Great Migration in the Serengeti and the wonders of Ngorongoro Crater to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro and the white sands of Zanzibar: a journey of a lifetime.

Explore Tanzania

Kenya

The Spirit of the Safari.

From the Great Migration in the Maasai Mara and elephants with Kilimanjaro views in Amboseli to the pink flamingos of Lake Nakuru and the white sands of Diani Beach: wild, vibrant and unforgettable.

Explore Kenya

Uganda

The Pearl of Africa.

From gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and the mighty Murchison Falls to the tree-climbing lions of Queen Elizabeth and the calm beauty of Lake Bunyonyi: pure, green and truly unforgettable.

Culture, History and HeritageConnect with the people who make Africa unforgettable.Visit the Vadoma two-toed tribe, explore Great Zimbabwe, and enjoy village tours, traditional dance, local food and craft markets, Robben Island, Bo-Kaap, Zulu heritage and Maasai culture: authentic experiences that respect and support communities. Adventure and ThrillsFor travellers who want their heart racing.White-water rafting on the Zambezi, bungee jumping at Victoria Falls, zip-lining, canoeing, climbing Mount Kilimanjaro, hiking Table Mountain and paragliding in Cape Town: adventure at its wildest. Beach and IslandWhite sand, turquoise water and total relaxation.Unwind in Zanzibar, Diani Beach, Bazaruto, Lake Malawi, Durban’s Golden Mile and Umhlanga, or on a Lake Kariba houseboat: the perfect end to any safari. Gorilla and PrimateA once-in-a-lifetime encounter in the misty forests.Trek mountain gorillas in Bwindi, track chimpanzees in Kibale, and meet colobus monkeys in Zanzibar with experienced local guides. Luxury and HoneymoonPrivate lodges, sunset cruises and unforgettable moments for two.Enjoy fly-in safaris, spa treatments, bush dinners under the stars and personal service made just for you.
