    Travel that gives back

    Malachite's work does not stop when a trip ends. The company supports children's education in rural Kariba and takes part in conservation work around the Lower Zambezi.

    The Smiling Children of MalaThe Mala Charity Foundation helps pay school fees and provides uniforms, learning materials and playing materials for disadvantaged children in rural Kariba.
