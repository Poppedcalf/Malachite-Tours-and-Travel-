Lake Kariba

Houseboat charters on Africa's great inland sea

Sail the lake on a Malachite houseboat charter, with the Matusadona Mountain Range, abundant wildlife and fantastic fishing along the way.

  • The lakeSome 280 km of water, with sunsets behind drowned trees and elephants swimming at dawn.
  • Houseboat chartersCruise on a private houseboat and sip sundowners as hippos surface around you.
  • Walking safaris and island campingDisembark for a walking safari, or camp on an island.
  • Tiger fish and breamTry your hand at catching the famous tiger fish and bream.
