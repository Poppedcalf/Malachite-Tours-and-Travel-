Travel with purpose

Part of every booking supports children and wildlife in the Kariba district. You can add a donation when you request a quote.

The Smiling Children of Mala

Run through the Mala Charity Foundation, this programme pays primary school fees, buys supplies and builds support for vulnerable children in rural Kariba.

Wildlife rehabilitation

Our vulture rehabilitation work protects birds that signal poaching and keep the valley healthy. We also work with the Tashinga Initiative, Zambezi Elephant Fund, Kariba Animal Welfare Fund Trust and Wild Zambezi.
