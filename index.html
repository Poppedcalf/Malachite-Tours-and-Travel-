Destinations

Zimbabwe, Southern and East Africa

Malachite Travel & Tours is proudly based in Zimbabwe, offering unforgettable packages to the most loved tourism hotspots across Zimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda.

  • 01

    Zimbabwe

    The Heart of Africa’s Wonder.From the mighty Victoria Falls and the wildlife of Hwange to the ancient Great Zimbabwe and the peaceful waters of Lake Kariba: a land of wonder, warmth and unforgettable adventure.Explore Zimbabwe
  • 02

    South Africa

    One Country, A World of Wonders.From the iconic Table Mountain and vibrant Cape Town to the Big Five in Kruger National Park and the scenic Garden Route: history, wildlife and breathtaking beauty all in one journey.Explore South Africa
  • 03

    Botswana

    Where the Wild Still Roams Free.From the winding waterways of the Okavango Delta to the elephant herds of Chobe, experience untouched wilderness, luxury safaris and nature at its purest.Explore Botswana
  • 04

    Tanzania

    Where Legends Are Born.From the Great Migration in the Serengeti and the wonders of Ngorongoro Crater to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro and the white sands of Zanzibar: a journey of a lifetime.Explore Tanzania
  • 05

    Kenya

    The Spirit of the Safari.From the Great Migration in the Maasai Mara and elephants with Kilimanjaro views in Amboseli to the pink flamingos of Lake Nakuru and the white sands of Diani Beach: wild, vibrant and unforgettable.Explore Kenya
  • 06

    Uganda

    The Pearl of Africa.From gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and the mighty Murchison Falls to the tree-climbing lions of Queen Elizabeth and the calm beauty of Lake Bunyonyi: pure, green and truly unforgettable.Explore Uganda

Experiences

Choose Your African Experience

  • 01

    Safari, Wildlife and Wilderness

    Get up close with the Big Five in Africa’s most iconic parks.From game drives in Hwange and the Serengeti to walking safaris in Mana Pools and night drives in Kruger, every safari is guided by experts who know the bush.See safari packages
  • 02

    Culture, History and Heritage

    Connect with the people who make Africa unforgettable.Visit the Vadoma two-toed tribe, explore Great Zimbabwe, and enjoy village tours, traditional dance, local food and craft markets, Robben Island, Bo-Kaap, Zulu heritage and Maasai culture: authentic experiences that respect and support communities.See culture packages
  • 03

    Adventure and Thrills

    For travellers who want their heart racing.White-water rafting on the Zambezi, bungee jumping at Victoria Falls, zip-lining, canoeing, climbing Mount Kilimanjaro, hiking Table Mountain and paragliding in Cape Town: adventure at its wildest.See adventure packages
  • 04

    Beach and Island

    White sand, turquoise water and total relaxation.Unwind in Zanzibar, Diani Beach, Bazaruto, Lake Malawi, Durban’s Golden Mile and Umhlanga, or on a Lake Kariba houseboat: the perfect end to any safari.See beach and houseboat packages
  • 05

    Gorilla and Primate

    A once-in-a-lifetime encounter in the misty forests.Trek mountain gorillas in Bwindi, track chimpanzees in Kibale, and meet colobus monkeys in Zanzibar with experienced local guides.See the Uganda package
  • 06

    Luxury and Honeymoon

    Private lodges, sunset cruises and unforgettable moments for two.Enjoy fly-in safaris, spa treatments, bush dinners under the stars and personal service made just for you.Plan a trip for two
