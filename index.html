Zimbabwe, Southern and East Africa

Malachite Travel & Tours is based in Kariba, Zimbabwe, and offers packages in Zimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda.

Zimbabwe

Photographic safaris, Victoria Falls, Lake Kariba houseboat charters, Mana Pools and Matusadona, and the heritage of Great Zimbabwe and the Matobo Hills.

Six packagesExplore Zimbabwe

South Africa

The Cradle of Humankind, Kruger and the Blyde River Canyon, Durban's beaches, and Cape Town with Robben Island and Table Mountain.

Four packagesExplore South Africa

Botswana

The Okavango Delta, the vast elephant herds of Chobe National Park, and Maun, the gateway to the Okavango.

7 nights / 8 daysExplore Botswana

Tanzania

The annual wildebeest and zebra migration in the Serengeti, then the beaches and spice farms of Zanzibar.

8 nights / 9 daysExplore Tanzania

Kenya

The Maasai Mara and the Great Wildebeest Migration, then Diani and Mombasa Beach on the coast.

7 nights / 8 daysExplore Kenya

Uganda

Gorilla and chimpanzee tracking, then Lake Bunyonyi and its archipelago of 29 islands.
