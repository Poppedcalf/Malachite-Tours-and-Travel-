About Malachite

A Kariba-based travel company for individuals, families and groups, with journeys across Zimbabwe, Southern Africa and East Africa.

Who we are

Malachite Travel & Tours offers travel experiences across Zimbabwe, Southern Africa and East Africa, for wildlife enthusiasts, history lovers and cultural explorers alike.

Our office is in Kariba, on the shore of the lake we know best. From here we plan canoe and walking safaris on the Zambezi, houseboat stays on Lake Kariba, weeks among Zimbabwe's ancient stone cities, and longer routes into South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda.

Our guides fill every stage of a journey with adventure, knowledge and memories worth keeping.

  • Where to find usStand 643, ZV Mall, Kariba, Zimbabwe
  • Where we travelZimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda
  • Who we serveIndividual travellers, families and groups, including corporate groups

What we arrange

From a single night on the lake to a multi-country route, planned around the way you want to travel.

  • Photographic safarisGame drives in Zimbabwe's national parks, including Hwange and Matusadona, and cruising on Lake Kariba.
  • History and cultureHeritage sites such as the Great Zimbabwe Ruins and the rock paintings of the Matobo Hills.
  • Museums and monumentsLandmarks including National Heroes Acre in Harare, the Bulawayo Railway Museum and the Natural History Museum.
  • Victoria FallsThe Falls, with day trips to Chobe National Park in Botswana and Livingstone in Zambia.
  • Lake Kariba houseboat chartersCruises past the Matusadona mountains, with walking safaris, island camping and tiger-fish and bream fishing.
  • Multi-country packagesFifteen packages across Zimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda.
  • TransfersSeamless transfers by air, water and road, so every part of the journey is hassle-free.
