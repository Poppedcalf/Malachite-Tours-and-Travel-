About Malachite

A Kariba-based travel partner for individuals, families and corporate groups.

Who we are

Malachite is a Kariba-based travel partner. We shape safari, lake and cultural journeys across Zimbabwe and Southern Africa around the way you want to travel.

From our base in Kariba we look after individual travellers, families and large corporate groups, whether the journey is a canoe along the Zambezi, a houseboat on the lake or a week among Zimbabwe's ancient stone cities.

Our line is simple: Malachite Travel and Tours brings a smile to your face.

  • Where to find usStand 643, ZV Mall, Kariba, Zimbabwe
  • Who we serveIndividual travellers, families and corporate groups
  • Where we workZimbabwe and Southern Africa, with East African journeys on request

Travel with purpose

The Zambezi Valley is both our workplace and our neighbourhood. Part of how we work is to support the children and wildlife of the Kariba district, and to support the organisations protecting this landscape.

The Smiling Children of MalaRun under the Mala Charity Foundation, helping with primary school fees, supplies and support for vulnerable children in rural Kariba.
Vulture rehabilitation and wildlife protectionProtecting endangered vultures in the Zambezi Valley. They are ecological indicators and help detect poaching.
Conservation organisations we supportThe Tashinga Initiative (ranger welfare and anti-poaching), Zambezi Elephant Fund (elephant habitat), Kariba Animal Welfare Fund Trust (wildlife rescue) and Wild Zambezi (eco-conscious travel standards).

Contributions are optional. Choosing one only adds it to your inquiry. Nothing is collected on this website.
