Trips & Experiences

The journeys, activities and services we arrange, from a night on the lake to a week among ancient stone cities.

Experiences

Each experience can be part of a standard journey or built into a tailor-made one. Tell us what you would like and we will shape the rest.

Lake Kariba

Houseboat stays and charters

A flexible stay on the water, from one to seven nights, planned around your group. Spend a night or a week with the shoreline's wildlife drifting past. Private trips, family reunions and executive retreats are all welcome.

About Lake Kariba

Lake Kariba

On the water: wildlife viewing, cruises and fishing

Tender-boat wildlife viewing lets you explore the shoreline closer than the houseboat can go. Boat cruises and sundowners on the water, and tiger fishing with a guide where you choose to add it, round out the days.

Ask about a cruise

Matusadona and Mana Pools

Game drives and wildlife viewing

Wilderness game drives and close wildlife observation, with time in Matusadona National Park as part of the Lower Zambezi Expedition.

About the parks

Mana Pools

Canoeing and walking safaris

Evening canoeing on the Zambezi and guided walking safaris in Mana Pools. Photographic safaris, with game drives timed for good light in parks such as Hwange, Mana Pools and Gonarezhou, are arranged on request.

Ask about photographic safaris

Bulawayo, Matobo, Khami and Great Zimbabwe

Heritage, rock art and cultural tours

Museums, rock art and ruins that tell the long story of Zimbabwe: Bulawayo's Natural History Museum, the Khami Ruins, San rock art in the Matobo Hills and the stone structures of Great Zimbabwe, with specialist guides.

About the heritage sites

Victoria Falls, Chobe and Livingstone

Victoria Falls and cross-border day trips

A guided visit to the Falls, with single-day excursions into Chobe National Park in Botswana and to Livingstone in Zambia: three countries in one route.

About Victoria Falls

Kruger and Mpumalanga, South Africa

Kruger and the Blyde River Canyon

A four-night base package pairing safaris in Kruger with the viewpoints of the Blyde River Canyon: God's Window and Bourke's Luck Potholes, with optional river rafting or zip-lining. Exact inclusions are to be confirmed.

About the route

Uganda and Zanzibar, on request

Primate tracking and the Zanzibar coast

Gorilla and chimpanzee tracking in Uganda's rainforests, and coastal stays and spice-farm itineraries on Zanzibar, planned around your dates. These journeys are arranged on request; no itinerary or rate is shown until the team has prepared a proposal.
