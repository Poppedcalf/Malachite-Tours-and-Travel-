Matusadona

A rugged lakeside wilderness on the southern shore of Lake Kariba.

Wilderness beside the lake

Matusadona National Park lies along the southern side of Lake Kariba. Its landscape brings together the Matusadona hills, river valleys, woodland and open grassland.

Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority describes the park as being flanked by Lake Kariba to the north and the Ume and Sanyati rivers. Its varied terrain supports wildlife and birdlife across a rugged lakeside setting.

Matusadona appears in Malachite's published Lower Zambezi Expedition as a wilderness game-viewing destination. The route, access arrangements, accommodation and operating availability should be confirmed directly before travel.
