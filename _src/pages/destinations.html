Zimbabwe

Heritage of Zimbabwe

Museums, rock art and ruins that tell the long story of Zimbabwe.

Stone, rock art and living culture

A heritage journey linking Bulawayo's Natural History Museum, the Khami Ruins, San rock art in the Matobo Hills and the stone structures of Great Zimbabwe.

  • BulawayoThe Natural History Museum and an introduction to the region.
  • Khami RuinsExplored with a specialist guide.
  • Matobo HillsSan rock art and granite landscapes.
  • Great ZimbabweThe ruins explored in depth, over a full day and a final morning.

South Africa

Cities, canyons and big game

Four short packages that can be taken on their own or strung together.

From Gauteng to the Cape

South Africa packs human history, dramatic scenery and classic wildlife into short, easily combined stays. Pick one, or link several into a longer route.

  • Gauteng4 nightsThe Cradle of Humankind, the Apartheid Museum and a guided walk through Lesedi Cultural Village.
  • Mpumalanga and Kruger4 nightsSafaris in Kruger, then God’s Window, Bourke’s Luck Potholes and the Blyde River Canyon, with optional zip-lining or rafting.
  • Durban3 nightsThe Golden Mile and the south beaches, and marine life at uShaka Marine World.
  • Cape Town4 nightsRobben Island, the Table Mountain cable car, Cape Point, the Cape of Good Hope and wine tasting in Stellenbosch.

Botswana

Delta, elephants and the Khoisan

Eight days across the Okavango Delta, Chobe and Maun.

Botswana’s great wildlife regions

One of the world’s largest inland deltas, a national park known for its vast elephant herds, and a gateway town where you can meet the Khoisan, among the world’s earliest peoples.

  • Okavango DeltaOne of the world’s largest inland deltas, teeming with wildlife.
  • Chobe National ParkFamous for its extraordinarily vast elephant herds. Chobe is also a one-day excursion from Victoria Falls on the Trans Borders Pack.
  • MaunThe gateway to the Okavango, with cultural experiences among the indigenous Khoisan.

Tanzania

The Serengeti, then Zanzibar

Nine days pairing the plains with the Indian Ocean coast.

Where the herds move without borders

Witness the annual wildebeest and zebra migration across the Serengeti, then trade the plains for the beaches and spice farms of Zanzibar.

  • Serengeti National ParkThe annual wildebeest and zebra migration, moving across the Maasai Mara trans-frontier as nature has always done, without borders.
  • ZanzibarKnown for its beaches and spice farms.

Kenya

The Mara and the coast

Eight days from the Maasai Mara to the Indian Ocean.

Migration and Maasai culture, then white sand

Witness the Great Wildebeest Migration and the thriving Maasai culture, then finish on the coast, where clear water and pale sand are the reward.

  • Maasai MaraThe iconic Great Wildebeest Migration, and the uniquely thriving Maasai culture.
  • Diani and Mombasa BeachA coastal paradise with crystal-clear waters and pristine white sands, including the chance to swim with dolphins.

Uganda

Primates and a lake of islands

Six days built around Uganda’s endangered primates.

Into the forest, then onto the water

Track gorillas and chimpanzees through Uganda’s forests, then settle beside Lake Bunyonyi, whose name is often translated as the “place of many little birds”.

  • Gorilla and chimpanzee trackingExplore the rich biodiversity of Uganda’s forests while tracking these special and endangered primates.
  • Lake BunyonyiA picturesque lake with tranquil water and an archipelago of 29 islands.
