Mala Charity Foundation

The Smiling Children of Mala

“Securing a future for disadvantaged children in Kariba Rural District.”

In Zimbabwe, adequate education is not easy to reach for marginalised children, particularly those in remote areas. Many are unable to attend school, chiefly because of poverty.

Malachite Travel & Tours runs a charity programme in the rural Kariba district called The Smiling Children of Mala. It began with a growing awareness of the stark gap between children who can go to school and those who cannot.

  • School feesWe help pay school fees for children whose families cannot afford the cost.
  • UniformsBasic school necessities, so that a child is ready for the classroom.
  • Learning materialsMaterials to learn with.
  • Playing materialsMaterials to play with, because school is also where children play.

You can help. Support is welcome in any form, whether monetary or material. To ask how to contribute, or how support is used, speak to the team directly.

Green tourism

Environment and conservation

Alongside the charity work, Malachite takes part in conservation and environmental initiatives around Kariba and the Lower Zambezi.

01

Vulture rehabilitation

Our wildlife rehabilitation programme focuses on endangered species, particularly vultures, some of which are becoming extinct through habitat destruction and a shortage of food.

We collect food waste from businesses around town and use it to feed them.

02

Clean-up campaigns

We regularly organise community-based campaigns to clean up natural and tourism sites in the Lower Zambezi area.

{{photo community-cleanup|Five men stand behind a pile of full black rubbish bags on a dusty track|(min-width:900px) 30vw, 100vw}}A clean-up in progress
03

Tree planting

We work with local communities to plant trees in areas affected by deforestation, contributing to environmental restoration and biodiversity conservation.
