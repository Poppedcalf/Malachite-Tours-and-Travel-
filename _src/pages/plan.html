How planning works

  1. Choose a destination or experienceBrowse the journeys, or tell us what you have in mind.
  2. Share your plansDates, group size and preferences, in the planner or on WhatsApp.
  3. Discuss the detailsRouting, accommodation and final pricing, with Malachite.
  4. Confirm directlyArrangements are confirmed with the company, not on this website.

An inquiry is not a reservation. This website does not confirm accommodation, availability, payment or bookings. It helps you send a clear request to the Malachite team.
