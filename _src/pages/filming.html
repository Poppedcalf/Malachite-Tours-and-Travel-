Filming in Africa

Malachite has supported film and video productions, from location scouting to crew travel and support on the ground.

From scouting to wrap

How we help film and video productions.

  • Location scoutingWe know the landscapes, parks and towns we work in, and scout locations to match your brief.
  • Crew travel and accommodationFlights, hotels, lodges and camps for your cast and crew.
  • Transport on the groundTransfers by air, water and road between locations.
  • Local knowledgeGuides who know the bush, the people and the best light.
