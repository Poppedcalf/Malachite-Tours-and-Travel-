Clearance Checklist

Start six weeks before travel, and do not travel until you have Ministry clearance. Six steps, and we handle each of them with you. Open a step for the details.

1Ministry of Information ClearanceNeeded before you travel, and for your visa.

Apply through the Zimbabwe Embassy or directly to the Ministry, with a letter to the Permanent Secretary giving each person’s full name, gender and passport number, your organisation, designations and contacts, the assignment, exact dates and locations, and a copy of each press card.

2ZMC AccreditationPer project, at the Zimbabwe Media Commission in Harare on arrival.

Bring your clearance letter and two passport photographs per person, and carry your accreditation at all times while filming.

3Location PermitsParks, heritage sites, and consent for people and private places.

Wildlife and parks: a ZimParks permit, with extra fees for Hwange, Mana Pools, the Victoria Falls Rainforest and night or moonlight filming. Heritage: a National Museums and Monuments permit for Great Zimbabwe, the Matobo Hills, rock art sites and similar. Food, people and private places: written permission from the owner and signed consent and release forms, especially for children.

4Equipment (ZIMRA)Temporary import of your kit; it leaves with the crew.

A full equipment list with serial numbers and values, cleared through a bonded agent or a temporary import guarantee. Keep the list for exit: all equipment must leave with the crew.

5Drones (CAAZ)Separate authorisation; restricted in national parks.

Authorisation from the Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe, applied for in advance. Drone use is restricted in national parks.

6Visa and Crew DocumentsPassports, visa, crew list, flights and a local address.

Passports valid for at least six months with three empty pages, and a visa issued with the clearance letter. A crew list, flight details and an address in Zimbabwe.

Fees and processing times change. We confirm the current requirements, fees and timings in your quotation.
