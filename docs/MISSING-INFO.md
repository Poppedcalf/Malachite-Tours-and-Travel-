# Malachite website: information to confirm before launch

This is a working checklist, not a statement that any item is wrong. Confirm with the Managing Director before publishing or changing company information.

## Contact and booking
- Confirm primary business email and whether the secondary email is monitored.
- Confirm the current office address(es) and the preferred address to publish.
- Confirm primary telephone, WhatsApp and landline details.
- Confirm the preferred process and recipient for trip-planner enquiries.

## Prices and trip operations
- All sample itineraries are enquiry-led. Confirm what routes are currently offered.
- Confirm the availability, inclusions, suppliers, guiding arrangements and seasonal restrictions for each activity.
- Keep illustrative rates hidden until written, approved prices are supplied.
- Confirm houseboat capacities, vessel details, charter conditions and booking terms.

## Credentials and partnerships
- Confirm exact award titles, awarding bodies, award years and evidence.
- Confirm the nature and current status of any ZTA registration or affiliation.
- Confirm which partner relationships may be publicly named or displayed with logos.
- Confirm which charities or conservation organisations Malachite currently supports and how the support should be described.

## Guest reviews
- Obtain genuine review wording and the guest's permission before reproducing testimonials.
- Link to original public review pages where possible. Do not use placeholder quotes.

## Media rights and accuracy
- Confirm Malachite owns or has permission to use supplied photos and videos.
- Confirm the exact location of destination-specific photographs before adding location-specific captions.
- Replace generic or temporary stock media with authentic company imagery when available.

## International visitor information
- Check visa, border, health, safety, park access and entry information against current official sources immediately before publication.
- Link to authoritative sources and avoid presenting general information as individual travel advice.
