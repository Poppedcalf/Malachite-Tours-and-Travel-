## Optimisation applied

- New photos are exported at 640, 1200 and 1920 px wide (only the sizes the original supports), progressive JPEG, no metadata. Pages use `srcset`, so phones download the 640 or 1200 px file.
- Larger existing photos were recompressed (about 1.6 MB saved).
- Hero video: 14 s, silent, 3.8 MB for desktop and 1.5 MB for phones. Both are skipped for visitors with reduced-motion or data-saver on.
- Originals are not stored in the project. Keep your own copies.
